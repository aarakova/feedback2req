# Planning complex work

Follow AGENTS.md for shared repository policy and docs/DECISIONS.md for approved
project decisions, including DEC-001 through DEC-005.

Use a written plan when complexity, duration, or dependencies make ongoing
tracking useful. File count alone does not require a plan. Simple, well-defined
tasks need no separate plan file.

When a file helps, keep it in docs/plans/<task>.md. Use whatever structure makes
the work clear; a short outline is enough. Useful content includes:

- the intended outcome, authorized scope, and relevant approved sources;
- meaningful steps, dependencies, and decisions still needed;
- validation appropriate to the task and material risks.

Update the plan as work progresses or new facts change the approach. Make
blocking questions explicit, seek the needed clarification, and continue work
that does not depend on the answer. A plan does not approve its proposals.

For substantial approved architecture or contract changes, consider the impact
on related documentation, tests, and implementation. Track affected updates and
remaining inconsistencies at a level useful for the task.

When the work finishes, make the delivered result, validation performed, and
remaining problems or limitations clear in the plan or final response.
